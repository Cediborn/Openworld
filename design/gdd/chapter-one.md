# Chapter One: The Shattered Island

Design notes for the combat overhaul, the expanded Brickton, the quest system
and the open-world activities. Numbers live in `game/data/`; content is
generated by `tools/content/build_quests.py` and `tools/content/build_world.py`.

## Combat

**Explorer.** A three-swing combo (forehand diagonal, backhand rising cut,
two-handed overhead chop) authored on the explorer's own skeleton by
`tools/bake_anims.gd`. The old swing clip targeted another rig's bone names
and moved nothing, which is why the sword never visibly swung.

- Damage is dealt only while a swing is *live* (a window of each clip, in
  `combat.combo`), and only to creatures the blade's real path crosses
  (blade segment + the arc it swept since the last frame vs. the creature's
  body line). Hitting at the press, or through the explorer's back, is impossible.
- At the start of a swing the explorer turns to the most likely target (nearest
  hostile within 4.5 m roughly where you aim) and steps in if needed.
- Presses during a swing queue the next one; the chain point is 60% through.
- Dodge roll (Q): 0.62 s, invulnerable from 0.05 to 0.4 s.
- Block (hold right mouse): a hit from the front does 25%, with less knockback.
- Hit reaction, death and the ready stance are authored too; the sword stays in
  the right hand throughout (it is attached to the hand bone).

**Monsters.** Every hostile species has its own moves with wind-up, strike and
recovery (`attacks` in `creatures.json`):

| Species | Moves |
|---|---|
| Ice troll | overhead smash (ground warning), hook, punch |
| Lava golem | overhead smash, ground pound (area, warning), hook, punch |
| Mushroom beetle | horn lunge (charges 2.4 m), bite |
| Fire salamander | lunging bite, tail whip (area) |
| Frost spirit | claw rake, wailing lunge |
| Wolf | pounce (leaps 2.2 m), bite |
| Boar | tusk charge, gore |

A creature tracks you for most of the wind-up, then commits; damage resolves at
the impact moment only if you are still inside the blow's reach and arc (or
area), so a sidestep or a roll makes it miss. Nothing deals damage outside an
attack. The old biped arm rotations were sign-flipped (arms swung backwards
into a T-pose when roaring); fixed.

**Bosses** (`bosses.json`) add volley (shards that land on warning circles),
quake (ring around the boss), burrow (Beetle Queen tunnels under you), summon
and charge (warning line), plus phases at health thresholds.

## Brickton

From one street with 14 houses to a town of 41 buildings: market square (five
stalls, well, benches, the contract board), the Brickton Arms inn, Tilly's
shop, the Town Hall with its bell tower, a north and a south residential street
with gardens, the craft quarter (carpenter, tannery) and a fortified east gate.
Street centre-lines are baked into `data/town.json` and form the villagers'
walking graph, so nobody walks through a house.

43 townsfolk and regional NPCs with habits: patrolling guards (who fight
raiders in town), merchants at their stalls, craftsmen at work, strollers,
chatting pairs, travellers at the inn tables. Far villagers animate at a third
of the rate; on phones only the nearest 18 are awake.

## Quests

Engine: `game/scripts/quests.gd` (steps of goals; talk, talk to N people,
reach, examine, collect, have, deliver, place, kill, boss, defend waves,
escort, observe, wait for night, night encounters). Rewards are paid once;
progress saves; a step's world entities are rebuilt after loading, so nothing
can get permanently stuck. Markers: `!` over people with a quest, `?` over
people to report to, and an on-screen marker for the tracked quest only.

Main story (after "The Island's Heart"): A Strange Silence → The Frozen
Expedition → Beneath the Ashes → The Glowing Heart → The Five Fragments → The
Awakening. Side: The Lost Merchant, The Blacksmith's Request, A Hunter's
Challenge, The Missing Caravan, The Herbalist's Collection, The Forgotten
Explorer, Trouble at the Gates, The Hidden Treasure, A Strange Crystal, The
Golem's Secret. Special: The Night of the Beasts, The Last Journal, The Queen
Beneath, The Forgotten Kingdom, The Stranger at Midnight.

The brief's five mission types map onto these: monster hunting (The Missing
Caravan, A Hunter's Challenge), town protection (Trouble at the Gates),
exploration (A Strange Crystal), NPC assistance (The Lost Merchant), boss
(The Queen Beneath).

## Activities

`game/scripts/world_features.gd`: discoveries (22), treasure (12), shrines (6:
order, align, guardian, components, hidden switches, secret route), rare
bosses (Cinderhulk, Gloomhorn, Rimeclaw, the Hollow Sentinel), monster
contracts (7 templates, 3 tiers), world events (raids, travelling merchant,
rare creatures, crystal surge, eruption, traveller in need). Events never start
during a quest battle or boss fight and only one runs at a time.
